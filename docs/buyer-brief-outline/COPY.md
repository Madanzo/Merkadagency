## Start with a short website brief

Copy this outline into your own document or email. Replace the brackets with what you know; write “to decide” for open questions. You do not need a finished brief to contact Camilo.


- Business and visitor:  [Business name], [current services] and [intended customers or service area]. Visitors need to understand [main question] and be able to [next action].

- Starting point:  New website: [why a site is needed]. Redesign: [current URL], [useful content to keep] and [specific problem to solve].

- Essential work and later ideas:  [Pages or visitor tasks needed first], [optional ideas] and [external tools that still need availability and cost confirmation].

- Content and decisions:  [Copy and permitted images already available], [content still needed] and [person who approves the work].

- What to preserve and hand over:  [Existing URLs or content to retain], [domain and hosting ownership] and [questions about editing or later changes]. List access questions; never include passwords.

- Project questions:  [Preferred timing and reason], [budget range, if you choose to share it] and [questions about scope, reviews, testing or ongoing work]. These are discussion inputs, not an agreed price or deadline.


### Two starting points to adapt

Planning examples only — not client projects or promised deliverables.

New website:  We need to explain [service] to [audience] and make [contact action] easy to find. We have [content] ready. Please discuss which pages are needed and who will supply the missing content before quoting.

Redesign:  Our site at [URL] has useful [pages] to keep, but visitors struggle with [specific task]. Please separate content or navigation changes from new functions, and agree any URL moves before replacing pages.


Email Camilo your brief or open questions, or review the scope and deliverables to discuss. The detailed questions below can help you fill the gaps; they are not prerequisites for an inquiry.
