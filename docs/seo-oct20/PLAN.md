## Schema and editorial rules

Organization, Service and BreadcrumbList JSON-LD describe visible facts only. No address, review, rating, price, customer, certification or result markup. Article/HowTo and FAQ enhancement are deferred; the visible questions still work without rich-result markup. Case-study schema is withheld with the unapproved material. No special “AI schema” or AI recommendation promise.

Every future page needs one canonical topic owner, a useful distinct answer, documented business-fact sources and an accountable reviewer. Camilo is the current commercial/content approval owner, not an assertion that he has reviewed every new paragraph. Source links, publication/update dates and bylines must reflect actual events and authorship. AI-assisted drafts require human factual/permission review. Recheck claims on change and before republishing; remove stale claims instead of changing dates alone. Customer names/assets/results require original evidence and explicit permission. No invented pricing, deadlines, guarantees, reviews or free-CRM availability.
